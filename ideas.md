# Cellova Labs Design Direction

## Style Decisions

- **Clinical research dossier:** Warm lab-white surfaces, charcoal instrument-black panels, thin verification rules, structured batch-record layouts, and restrained scientific green accents distinguish Cellova Labs from consumer wellness storefronts.
- **Typography:** Editorial display headlines are paired with letterspaced technical micro-labels, badges, table headers, and process labels to convey laboratory documentation and control.
- **Verification green:** `#64A72C` and its approved logo-derived accents signal proof, batch status, primary actions, and key numerals—not general decoration.
- **Record-first catalog language:** Collection and COA states use thin rules, explicit record status, lot/specification context, and neutral documentation-first copy rather than consumer-promo language.
- **Cohesion rule:** Product and collection surfaces retain the warm-white/charcoal dossier foundation; green remains reserved for verified data, numerical checkpoints, status, and primary actions.
