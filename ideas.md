# Cellova Labs Storefront — Design Direction

## Three visual approaches

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm Laboratory Editorial | An information-first research storefront pairing calm paper surfaces with structured dark panels and controlled amber energy. It feels validated, readable, and confident rather than sales-driven. | 0.07 |
| Field Notebook System | A softer archival system using specimen-label typography, measured annotations, and tactile paper-like composition. It makes documentation feel discoverable and human. | 0.03 |
| Calibrated Instrument | A high-contrast scientific instrument interface with dense monochrome data panels, clearly segmented controls, and a narrow amber indicator. It feels technical and purposeful. | 0.09 |

## Selected approach: Warm Laboratory Editorial

### Design Movement

**Contemporary scientific editorial**: a restrained, premium interpretation of laboratory documentation, combining generous Paper surfaces with the structured reliability of Ink and Indigo. The approach uses Cellova’s supplied visual system directly rather than borrowing a prior storefront’s visual language.

### Core Principles

1. **Information earns attention.** Product identity, specifications, status, and documentation retain a clear reading order.
2. **Warmth is controlled.** Spark is meaningful and scarce: it directs actions and confirms the brand, but never overpowers the page.
3. **Technical details are legible.** Metadata is visibly technical without becoming small, crowded, or inaccessible.
4. **Restraint signals confidence.** A decisive layout, ample Paper space, and a singular focal point replace hype and visual noise.

### Color Philosophy

Paper (`#F3F4F1`) leads the visual field to create a calm, clinical baseline. Ink (`#12141C`) is the authoritative structural color for navigation, legal content, footers, and primary reading contrast. Indigo (`#2D3452`) organizes major surfaces and display content. Slate (`#8B93A7`) carries borders and secondary technical details. Spark (`#F2A63C`) is Cellova’s controlled energy: the logo, key actions, and small numbering accents only.

### Layout Paradigm

Pages unfold as a **research dossier** rather than a uniform marketing grid. A narrow utility bar introduces the site; a precision header follows; the homepage opens with an asymmetric information sheet that overlays an art-directed product field. Sections alternate between broad Paper ledgers and dark archival panels. Product detail pages retain specifications and lot documentation in the first visual band.

### Signature Elements

1. A slim, controlled **Spark calibration line** that marks focal boundaries and primary actions.
2. **Dossier labels** set in IBM Plex Mono for product class, SKU, lot, and documentation status.
3. The **Paper panel / Indigo field** relationship: pale information sheets resting against an Indigo stage.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interaction confirms intent rather than seeking attention. Controls lift only slightly and use decisive Ink focus rings. A cart or account action should reveal its status clearly and immediately. Primary navigation becomes a compact, readable layer on mobile without changing its information hierarchy.

### Animation

Nonessential entrances use short, staggered opacity-and-translate transitions (160–240 ms, `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`). Buttons press to `scale(0.97)` over 140 ms and avoid decorative loops. The calibration line can reveal horizontally on initial page entry. All motion is disabled for reduced-motion preferences; no layout-affecting animation is used.

### Typography System

**Sora 700** carries display headlines, product names, and decisive navigation moments. **Inter 400–700** supports body, buttons, form labels, and navigation. **IBM Plex Mono 500** identifies technical values and labels. Headlines use sentence case and spacious leading; technical labels use uppercase with restrained tracking. Spark is not used as small body text on Paper.

### Brand Essence

**Cellova Labs is a research-focused storefront for teams who need clear specifications, organized documentation, and a confident path from catalog to COA.**

**Personality:** Clear, precise, measured.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are short, factual, and outcome-oriented. Calls to action are direct and specific; microcopy explains the next step without over-promising. Avoid medical claims, urgency language, broad superlatives, and filler.

> “Precision you can verify.”

> “Review lot documentation.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The supplied **Cellova Labs** wordmark is used without recreation or distortion. It receives at least the specified digital clear space and appears in its amber primary or approved monochrome/reversed variants. A standalone abstract calibration-mark icon, rather than generated wordmark typography, will be used for the favicon only.

**Spark — `#F2A63C`**
